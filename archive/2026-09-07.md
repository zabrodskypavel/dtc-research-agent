## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: Momentous

*(Runner-ups considered: **MaryRuth's Organics** — strong 2025 DTC growth, but multiple trackers show 2026 revenue projected to decline 20–50%, a deceleration rather than a standout trend. **Other Half Pet** — a pet-supplement candidate, but only a $3M seed with no disclosed revenue/scale, too early-stage to call an outlier.)*

**Momentous** landed at **No. 426 on the 2026 Inc. 5000** with **821% three-year revenue growth** — its fourth consecutive year on the list. Founded in 2016 by former NFL lineman Jeff Byers and Rob Dyrdek (Park City/Jackson-based), the brand's growth story rests on third-party rigor rather than influencer hype: all 50+ SKUs are NSF Certified for Sport, and the company says it spent over $1M in 2025 on testing/certification, scrapping non-compliant lots of tongkat ali, ashwagandha, and its collagen shot (a company self-report, not independently audited).

**Why it's grown:** A 2022 multi-year Huberman Lab podcast partnership (Dr. Andrew Huberman as scientific advisor) is widely credited with an early step-change in sales, though that catalyst predates this year's growth window. More recently, Momentous announced an exclusive nationwide rollout into The Vitamin Shoppe/Super Supplements (640+ stores, Jan 2026), shifting from DTC-only toward omnichannel.

**Acquisition channels and tactics:** Research-credibility as a growth engine — DoD SBIR grants studying collagen for injury prevention, a $500K Women in Sport Science research grant, and direct B2B supply to 200+ pro/collegiate teams and NFL clubs (it became USA Rugby's official supplement partner in 2025). This team-supply/credibility channel is a genuine differentiator versus typical influencer-led DTC supplement brands.

**Retention/loyalty tactics:** Subscription DTC layered on the research-credibility flywheel rather than a points/rewards program.

**Primary marketing channels:** Podcast partnership (Huberman Lab) as the original unlock; increasingly, retail merchandising and B2B/team-supply credibility rather than paid social.

**Geographic markets:** Primarily US; international limited to distributor partners in the UK (Vielo, Healf) and Canada (Cascade Gear, HLC), with stated plans to expand into Europe/Asia.

**What makes the business model unique:** Full-catalog third-party sport certification (NSF Certified for Sport across the entire line, not just flagship SKUs) combined with direct-to-team B2B distribution as both a sales channel and a credibility signal — a structurally different go-to-market than the typical creator-led DTC supplement playbook.

**Balance note:** Funding is modest relative to the growth claimed (~$43.6M total; a 2024 strategic round from Humble Growth). Trustpilot reviews are polarized — recurring complaints on premium pricing (~$1/serving above category average for whey isolate) and subscription/international shipping friction. No FTC action or lawsuit found. Total current-year revenue could not be independently verified — treat the growth story as rate-of-change evidence (821% 3-year, Inc. 5000-verified), not an absolute revenue claim.
