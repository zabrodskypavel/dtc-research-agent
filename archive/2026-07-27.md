## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: Create Wellness

*(IM8 was last week's spotlight and Holy was the leading alternative candidate — re-evaluated this week and displaced: Holy's freshest hard datapoint is a forward-looking UK spend commitment, not a growth result, its most-cited 2025 revenue figure traces only to an unverifiable AI-generated wiki, and its last real funding event was 2023. Create Wellness has multiple fresh, cross-verified, dated proof points inside the trailing 6–12 months.)*

Create Wellness makes creatine monohydrate **gummies** — a format manufacturers reportedly told founders Dan and Sienna McCormick (ex-Away, Parade, Not Boring; ex-Goldman Sachs) wasn't chemically feasible at an effective dose. Solving that gave the brand a genuine "category creator" story, founded December 2022.

**Why it's growing:** a nationwide retail rollout into **Target, The Vitamin Shoppe, and Sprouts** (October 2025) made it the top-selling creatine gummy at each retailer since launch. That traction fed a **$20M Series B** (announced March 30, 2026; Alliance Consumer Growth led, with Mike Repole's Impact Capital and existing investor Unilever Ventures — prior raise was a $5M Series A in 2024). The company has now sold 250M+ gummies (~83M servings), scaling from 5 full-time staff in late 2025 to 25 today, with $30–40M in 2026 marketing spend planned. A new "Sour Grape" flavor launched July 15, 2026, showing continued in-market momentum.

**Acquisition channels:** influencer/UGC seeding via agency Kynship — ~1,000 relevant creators identified, seeded free product with no-strings messaging, gifted founders and wellness micro-influencers (25K+ followers); Kynship reports 263% new-customer revenue growth attributable to the program. Paid spend splits roughly evenly across TikTok and Meta.

**Retention/loyalty tactics:** subscription model with discounts and larger pack sizes to offset per-unit price objections; limited-edition SKUs and gift-with-purchase promos (e.g. Black Friday) used to drive repeat purchase.

**Marketing channels:** TikTok, Meta, influencer/UGC, retail merchandising (Target, Vitamin Shoppe, Sprouts, GNC, Wegmans, Amazon), DTC site.

**Geographic markets:** US-only — no international expansion reported yet; omnichannel between DTC and mass/specialty retail.

**What makes the model unique:** first-mover on a gummy format for a supplement (creatine) that's traditionally powder/capsule, which both solved a real consumer pain point (taste/mixing) and created defensible retail-planogram positioning as "the" creatine gummy.

**Balance note:** a class action (Connolly v. Create Wellness, filed April 22, 2026, SDNY) alleges gummies contain ~10% less creatine than the labeled 4.5g/serving; Create disputes this, citing third-party Eurofins potency testing.
