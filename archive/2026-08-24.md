## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: Micro Ingredients

*(Runner-ups considered: **Apothékary** — $16M Series A with a $40M 2026 sales target, but the target is forward-looking, not a landed trailing result. **Rae Wellness** — $14M Series A with TikTok Shop traction, but no comparably fresh, dated revenue disclosures.)*

Micro Ingredients — a Diamond Bar, CA supplement brand founded ~2015–16 by Sara Lyu, originally an Amazon-first organic-spice/superfood-powder seller now expanding into collagen, ashwagandha, mushroom, and fiber supplements — posted the strongest verifiable trailing-6–12-month growth story found this cycle, built on multiple cross-verified 2026 data points rather than a single stale headline.

**Why it's growing:** It's the **#1 supplement brand on TikTok Shop**, generating ~$31.2M in sales year-to-date (Charm.io data via WWD/Dealroom/NutraIngredients, all reporting the same figures within days of each other, late June 2026) — ahead of MaryRuth's ($27.9M) and Goli Nutrition ($20M). It has passed **$111.7M in cumulative TikTok Shop GMV** (3.2M units, 117K+ creator videos), after crossing $50M cumulative in Oct 2024 and $63.4M for full-year 2024 — growth has visibly accelerated into 2026, not decelerated. February 2026 alone: $6.59M with 13.3% growth, re-entering the platform's top 10.

**Acquisition channels:** Almost entirely social commerce — TikTok Shop livestream selling plus a broad affiliate/creator program (nano-influencers through larger "faceless" product-showcase accounts) using transformation-story and comparison hooks. This layers on its original Amazon channel, where it's a top-8,000 US seller with 50,000+ ratings and a 4.9-star average.

**Retention/model:** No visible subscription-first model (unlike Ritual/IM8) — it wins on SKU breadth, single-ingredient "raw and pure" positioning, and constant creator-content volume rather than locked-in recurring billing, a genuinely different model from most DTC supplement peers in this library.

**Geography:** US-only evidence found (Amazon + TikTok Shop US); no confirmed international retail.

**Marketing channels:** Creator/affiliate-driven TikTok Shop content plus Amazon SEO/reviews — no major paid-media or celebrity-endorsement presence found, unlike most brands profiled in this section to date.

**Balance note — notably weaker than most anchors profiled so far:** Trustpilot rating a mediocre 3.3/5 (missing items, unresponsive support, quality/authenticity concerns tied partly to third-party Amazon resellers). Its legal entity, DPL Trading Inc., faces an active class action (*Willis-Albrigo v. DPL Trading, Inc.*, filed Sept 19, 2025, C.D. Cal.) alleging false "10g protein" labeling on its Pure Peanut Powder, plus a separate California Prop 65 60-day notice over lead/cadmium in powdered products. No FDA recall issued. Total company revenue beyond the disclosed platform-specific TikTok Shop/Amazon figures is not publicly disclosed — no overall-revenue number is claimed here.
