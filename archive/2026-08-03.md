## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: Arrae

*(Runner-ups considered: Holy — real UK push and Symrise tie-up, but the freshest hard evidence is forward-looking ("expansion is a key focus in 2026") rather than a landed trailing-12-month result, so passed on again. Unilever's Grüns acquisition is outlier-scale but thin on fresh post-acquisition operating detail to fill out six bullets.)*

Arrae is a women's wellness/gut-health-and-mood supplement brand founded by husband-and-wife team Siff Haider and Nish Samantray in March 2020 (Toronto/NYC). It crossed **$100M in revenue** with retail distribution growth exceeding **460% year-to-date**, per a Forbes feature (Feb 18, 2026).

**Why it's growing:** A tight, narrow product line (~8–10 "hero" SKUs targeting gut, cortisol/stress, mood, and menopause) reached nine figures without broad catalog sprawl. Retail did the heavy lifting recently: nationwide at **Sprouts Farmers Market since August 2025** (top-selling women's supplement in Sprouts' innovation-center program) and **Target nationwide since ~October 2025**. In September 2025 the brand signed **Pamela Anderson** as a partner/face for a deliberate push into midlife/perimenopause positioning, extending beyond its Gen Z/Millennial TikTok base.

**Acquisition channels:** Organic and paid TikTok/Instagram creator content is the core engine — a founder-described "democratized" influencer approach treating regular customers like VIPs. The original breakout came from one friend's organic post, taking Arrae from $0 to $40K/month within its first year. Retail end-caps at Sprouts and Target now layer on as a secondary discovery channel.

**Retention/loyalty:** A tiered subscription program offers up to 20% off, free US/Canada shipping, and "Arrae Cash" back (5/10/15%, scaling with tenure) — with a dedicated Senior Retention Manager role, signaling institutionalized CRM investment.

**Marketing channels:** Creator/UGC social (dominant), celebrity-anchored earned media (Anderson), growing retail merchandising, founder-led podcast/press circuit.

**Geography:** DTC ships US/Canada/international; physical retail is US-only so far.

**Model uniqueness:** Reached $100M+ largely **bootstrapped** — self-funded from savings to $1M revenue within ~8 months, no capital raised until a comparatively small seed round later (Vanterra Ventures, Backbone Angels, others) — an unusually capital-light path versus VC-fueled peers.

**Balance note:** Trustpilot score is weak (**1.9/5, 145+ reviews**) — recurring complaints on product efficacy, subscription/billing transparency, and slow shipping. No lawsuit or FTC action specific to Arrae found, though FTC scrutiny is active elsewhere in the cortisol-supplement subcategory it competes in.
