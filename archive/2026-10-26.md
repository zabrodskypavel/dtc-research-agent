## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: Applied Nutrition

Applied Nutrition (LSE: APN) is the UK sports-nutrition brand that went from a scaffolding apprentice's side hustle to a FTSE-250-adjacent, publicly-audited growth story — and unlike most of this brief's subjects, its numbers come from statutory filings, not founder-reported estimates.

**Why it's grown.** Founder Thomas Ryder built the brand in-house (UK manufacturing, Kirkby) after starting with Canada-sourced products, then took it public in October 2024. FY26 (year to July 31, 2026) revenue hit £160M, **+50% YoY**, beating consensus (£148.4M); adjusted EBITDA rose 40% to £43.3M. H1 FY26 revenue was already up 56.5% YoY, prompting two guidance raises during the year. FY27 guidance points to ~£205M revenue (+28%) and ~£49M adjusted EBITDA — guidance, not yet realized, flagged as such.

**Acquisition channels/tactics.** Not DTC-subscription-first — this is a retail/wholesale distribution business. UK growth runs through Holland & Barrett (a joint business plan that could triple H&B revenue FY24→FY26), Tesco, and Asda, with JD Sports as a 2021 minority investor. US entry is funded via a $16M acquisition of Nutrablend Group (Buffalo, NY manufacturing, up to $300M capacity) and a North American licensing deal with Mondelej (Sour Patch Kids/Swedish Fish-branded sports nutrition), rolling into ~2,200 Walmart and ~1,300 GNC stores (full GNC availability Sept 1, 2026).

**Retention/loyalty.** No subscription or cohort-retention data exists to report — and that's itself notable: this brand's model is built on retail shelf presence and brand-ambassador equity, not a DTC retention loop. Coleen Rooney is both a brand ambassador and an equity holder who increased her shareholding in Feb 2026 — an equity-aligned endorsement rather than a flat fee.

**Marketing channels.** Celebrity/ambassador-led (Rooney for wellness/collagen/greens lines), hyper-regional US marketing credited by The Grocer for cracking a historically tough US sports-nutrition market, and a licensing arm (Blonde Sheep Licensing) that turns the brand into a platform for co-branded products. Hired ex-THG CMO Sophie Pugh (Oct 2025) to run global marketing.

**Geography.** UK core, aggressive 2026 US/Canada expansion now the primary growth vector.

**What makes the model unique.** A bootstrapped, non-DTC, retail-and-licensing growth engine with audited public-company financials — a useful contrast to the subscription-DTC playbook most of this brief's brands run.

**Balance note.** Trustpilot ~4.5/5 across ~4,200 reviews, overwhelmingly positive (occasional delivery/customs complaints only); no lawsuits or FTC action found.
