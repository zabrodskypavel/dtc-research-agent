## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: AG1

AG1 (formerly Athletic Greens) is the daily-greens powder that built the template every "foundational nutrition" brand since has copied — and after years of guarded financials, it's now visibly in motion: a live sale process, its first new product format in 16 years, and the biggest retail push in its history, all inside the last six months.

**Why it's grown.** AG1 scaled a single hero SKU into a category-defining brand via relentless podcast sponsorship — long-form ad reads rather than quick-hit paid social — paired with trusted-authority endorsers (athletes, doctors, performance coaches). Reuters reported April 10, 2026 that AG1 is exploring a sale or minority stake, with bankers (reportedly Goldman Sachs) pricing any deal above $2B including debt — a signal the growth story is drawing real acquirer interest, in the same window as Unilever's ~$1.2B purchase of Grüns and Danone's deal for Huel. Public revenue figures are thin (outside estimates: ~$150M in 2022 to ~$600M in 2024; no confirmed 2026 figure), so treat scale claims as directional, not audited.

**Acquisition channels.** Podcasts remain the primary driver, with Meta and TikTok used mainly to retarget rather than cold-acquire; the brand leans on long-term endorsement deals over one-off influencer spend.

**Retention/loyalty.** Subscription-first, with structured winback flows (email/SMS/direct mail) built around reminding lapsed customers why they started; cancel-flow surveys feed a retention team tracking cohort LTV.

**Marketing channels.** Podcast advertising, paid social for retargeting, and — new this year — TikTok Shop as a distribution channel for its gummy line.

**Geography.** US-core DTC, now layering in mass retail: Target (April 2026, doubling retail doors, first shelf placement for AGZ sleep product), Costco (mid-2025), The Vitamin Shoppe nationwide (January 2026), Amazon (2025). International push is thinner — its first-ever sports sponsorship (Swansea City FC, August 2026) is a UK brand play, not a confirmed international sales expansion.

**Business model.** The distinctive move is diversifying off a single hero SKU: AG1 Pro (creatine-added, June 2026), AGZ (sleep), and AG1 Essentials Gummies (August 2026, its first new format in 16 years, DTC + TikTok Shop) — turning a one-product funnel into a multi-format "wellness platform" while keeping subscription DTC as the backbone.

**Balance note.** A pending consumer class action (*Hoke et al. v. AG1*, C.D. Cal., filed Feb 2026) challenges subscription-disclosure and cancellation terms; no FTC action found. No Trustpilot data surfaced this run.

**Runner-ups considered:** Perelel (strong women's-health funding story, but freshest hard data is a Nov-2025 raise — stale per this brief's freshness rule); LMNT (large scale, no verifiable trailing-12-month growth figure).
