## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: MOSH

**MOSH**, a "brain health nutrition" bar and supplement brand co-founded in 2021 by Maria Shriver and her son Patrick Schwarzenegger (the name stands for "Maria Owings Shriver Health"), is the standout growth story of the trailing 6–12 months in functional nutrition. The brand closed a **$13M Series A led by Main Street Advisors in May 2026** to scale into national grocery, and its retail footprint is **on track to triple in 2026** — from roughly 2,000 doors at the start of the year toward a **5,000-door year-end target** — driven by an already-confirmed **Target launch (May 2026)** alongside existing placement at Sprouts, Albertsons, Kroger, and H-E-B.

**Why it's grown:** MOSH built two years of DTC-only proof-of-concept before touching retail — bars sold exclusively direct-to-consumer saw sales rise over 70% in each of its first two years, reaching roughly $12M in 2024 revenue on 2.5M+ bars sold, before the brand used that traction to negotiate its way into national grocery. The core hook is positioning nutrition bars as **cognitive health, not just protein** — a differentiated angle in a crowded bar category — reinforced by founder-market fit: Shriver has spent two decades as a public face of Alzheimer's advocacy (her father had the disease), giving the brand's mission real authenticity rather than a bolted-on cause.

**Acquisition channels and tactics:** DTC-first digital marketing and founder-driven media (Shriver/Schwarzenegger's own reach and press access) built the initial base; the brand then paired that with a dedicated ecommerce/growth agency (Avenue Z) for site optimization and paid-channel scaling as it approached retail.

**Retention/loyalty tactics:** Subscription-based DTC purchasing on the core bar line; specific tier/loyalty-program detail is thin in public sources.

**Primary marketing channels:** Founder-led earned media and press, paid digital, and now in-store retail merchandising as the newer growth lever — the classic "prove DTC, then leverage into big-box" sequencing also seen in Native Pet (2026-09-14 spotlight) and several other brands this brief has covered.

**Geographic markets:** US-only; no international expansion found. A same-named, unrelated Australian brand (getmosh.com.au) exists — distinct company, flagged to avoid conflation.

**What makes the business model unique:** A cause-linked model where a portion of every purchase funds Alzheimer's research at the Women's Alzheimer's Movement at Cleveland Clinic — a genuine mission tie-in rather than standard cause-marketing, given the founders' personal history — combined with a bar format (rather than powder/capsule) that lets it compete on grocery shelf real estate against mainstream protein bars while charging a premium for the cognitive-health positioning.

**Balance note:** Reviews are mixed — praise for taste/quality alongside complaints about subscription billing/customer-service responsiveness (consistent with the DTC-subscription growing pains seen across this brief's other spotlights). No lawsuits or FTC action found.
