## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: Vetnique Labs

**Vetnique Labs** — the Gryphon Investors–backed pet-health platform behind **Glandex** (anal gland/digestive), **YuMOVE** (joint mobility, acquired from UK's Lintbells in 2024) and **OticBliss** (ear care) — is in the middle of the fastest retail scale-up of its 13-year history, and the strongest verifiable pet-supplement growth story found this cycle.

**Why it's grown:** A private-equity-backed "roll-up" strategy is bundling several single-condition, vet-formulated supplement brands under shared retail and manufacturing infrastructure, riding the broader pet-wellness/humanization wave rather than leaning on one hero SKU. As of Sept 30, 2025 (the freshest hard milestone found, just inside the trailing-12-month window), the company had crossed **50,000+ US retail doors** — Walmart, Target.com, Tractor Supply, Petco, PetSmart, Pet Supplies Plus — with Walmart sales up triple digits YoY and 11 core SKUs across 3,563 Walmart stores. By early 2026 Glandex had pushed into 600+ physical Target stores and ~200 Pet Supermarket locations. In Feb 2026 the company brought in outside consumer-industry veteran Noel Geoffroy as Global CEO (founder Dr. James Bascharon moved to the board) and launched Glandex Max/YuMOVE MVS plus "VetPass," a formalized vet-sampling program debuted at VMX 2026.

**Acquisition channels/tactics:** Originated as a single Amazon listing, then vet-clinic seeding/sampling — now systematized via VetPass. Growth now leans on mass-retail door expansion plus continued Amazon/Chewy e-commerce gains.

**Retention/loyalty:** Standard Chewy/Amazon subscribe-and-save, plus cross-sell across life-stage and condition-specific SKUs within one household; no distinctive proprietary loyalty program found.

**Marketing channels:** Veterinarian endorsement and in-clinic distribution are the core trust moat, reinforced by retail merchandising — no confirmed large-scale influencer or paid-social campaign surfaced.

**Geography:** US-core (50,000+ doors) plus UK/Europe via YuMOVE, which calls itself the UK's #1 vet joint brand and is also the AKC's official joint supplement in the US.

**Business model:** A multi-brand, vet-channel-anchored supplement "platform" rather than a single-brand DTC play — PE ownership using M&A to stitch together condition-specific brands and share retail leverage.

**Balance note:** No lawsuits, FTC actions, or notable negative Trustpilot/BBB patterns found. Chewy reviews for flagship Glandex skew positive (~4.5/5 across 1,900+ ratings); complaints are limited to flavor mismatch and dosing hassle, not legal/safety issues. Caveat: growth metrics (door counts, triple-digit Walmart growth) are company self-reported via press release, not independently audited.
