## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: IM8

*(Grüns and Holy were both live alternative candidates this week — selected by growth-story strength, not geography. IM8 wins on scale and recency: a $1B raise less than two weeks ago, still generating fresh coverage.)*

IM8 is the health-drink brand built around David Beckham's public profile, operated by Prenetics — a Hong Kong-originated, Nasdaq-listed (PRE) health-tech company. That's unusual for the category: most DTC supplement brands are privately held, but IM8 sits inside a public company with real disclosure obligations, giving its growth numbers unusual transparency.

**Why it's growing:** the brand closed **$1 billion** in growth financing from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund (July 14, 2026) — a structure GC uses sparingly, which drove outsized press attention on its own. That capital follows real revenue traction: preliminary monthly revenue reached ~$17M in June 2026, prompting Prenetics to raise FY2026 IM8 revenue guidance to $210–220M; separate reporting has Prenetics projecting IM8 reaching ~$300M in annualized run-rate revenue by year-end 2026. A February 2026 product refresh (Daily Ultimate Essentials PRO, new flavors) kept the core "one daily formula" product current.

**Acquisition channels:** less performance-marketing, more halo marketing — Beckham's own global profile functions as always-on brand marketing, layered with major sponsorship: IM8 became the exclusive Official Health Supplements Partner of Inter Miami CF (May 2026, multi-year), a club Beckham co-owns, giving the brand a built-in, credible US distribution and visibility channel.

**Retention/loyalty tactics:** not confirmed from public sources — the "daily essentials" format implies a habitual, subscription-shaped product, but no explicit loyalty-program details surfaced. Flagging as unconfirmed rather than guessing.

**Marketing channels:** celebrity co-founder equity (Beckham), major sports sponsorship (Inter Miami CF), and a CSR partnership with Vitamin Angels (April 2026, committing to reach 400,000 mothers and children against malnutrition in year one) — a trust-building move alongside the commercial push.

**Geographic markets:** Hong Kong/Prenetics origin, UK profile via Beckham, clear US growth push evident in the Miami sponsorship and US-based General Catalyst backing.

**What makes the model unique:** the only brand in this week's set backed by a publicly traded parent, and the only one where the founder's personal global brand *is* the primary marketing channel rather than a paid endorsement layered on top.
