## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: IM8

*(Runner-ups considered: **Thorne** — P&G's $3.8B acquisition (Aug 4) is huge-outlier-scale, but the growth evidence everywhere is a multi-year retrospective CAGR, not a fresh trailing-6-month datapoint beyond the deal headline itself. **Grüns** — Unilever's $1.2B buy (April 2026) is outlier-scale but has no fresh post-acquisition operating metrics, the same staleness gap flagged in a prior run.)*

IM8, the David Beckham/Prenetics longevity-supplement brand, posted the fastest and best-documented growth curve of any brand assessed this cycle — its first full spotlight after being tracked weekly as an anchor since this project began.

**The numbers:** $120M ARR in December 2025 (12 months post-launch, +480% YoY); $33.8M Q1 2026 revenue (~6x YoY); ~$17M monthly revenue by May/June 2026; and on Aug 7, 2026, Prenetics raised FY2026 guidance to $210–220M (its second raise this year), targeting a $300M annualized run-rate by year-end.

**Why it's growing:** Clinical credibility (NSF Certified for Sport, a 12-week RCT, a Scientific Advisory Board including Mayo Clinic and ex-NASA figures) paired with Beckham's celebrity halo — the growth trajectory itself has become a recurring press story, reinforcing demand.

**Acquisition channels:** Heavy paid social/search (Meta, Google) across a 97-landing-page testing ecosystem. Standout tactic: a $1B non-dilutive "Customer Value Fund" financing deal with General Catalyst (closed July 14, 2026) that funds up to 70% of marketing spend per monthly cohort, repaid only from that cohort's revenue — a structure purpose-built for aggressive customer acquisition without diluting Prenetics shareholders.

**Retention/loyalty:** Quarterly subscriptions (20% off) launched in the US in Q4 2025, expanded internationally in Q1 2026; layered with a Rewards Club and VIP referral/ambassador program.

**Marketing channels:** Paid digital, an AI-native marketing/CX/merchandising stack, and sports sponsorship — an exclusive multi-year Inter Miami CF partnership (May 2026).

**Geography:** Sold in 43 countries (as of mid-2026, approximate); 60%+ of revenue generated outside the US.

**Model uniqueness:** The General Catalyst CVF deal is the standout feature — revenue-based, cohort-matched, non-dilutive financing rather than equity, letting Prenetics fund acquisition aggressively without shareholder dilution.

**Balance note:** ~4-star Trustpilot rating on volume, but recurring complaints on shipping delays, per-serving cost (~$3.30, ~$2.63 subscribed), and some reviewers reporting no noticeable effect. No lawsuits or FTC action found.
