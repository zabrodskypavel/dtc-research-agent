## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: Bloom Nutrition

*(Runner-ups considered: PetLab Co. — strong story, but the freshest hard evidence (BC Partners majority stake, $200M revenue figure) dates to Feb 2025, outside the trailing-12-month window, with no fresher 2026 numbers found. Momentous — fresh Jan 2026 nationwide Vitamin Shoppe rollout, but the underlying growth metrics being cited (revenue tripling, Inc. 5000 ranking) are pulled from 2023–2024 data, so only the retail news itself is current.)*

Bloom Nutrition, founded 2019 by fitness-influencer Mari Llewellyn and CEO Greg LaVecchia, started as a single-SKU greens-and-superfoods powder and has evolved into a multi-category beverage-and-wellness platform on pace for **~$500M in 2026 revenue**, with the company publicly framing its trajectory toward $1B (Forbes, Apr 28, 2026).

**Why it's growing:** New products are landing fast and big. Bloom Sparkling Energy (launched 2024, nationwide early 2025) reportedly scaled to an eight-figure business within six months; Colostrum & Collagen Peptides sold out in four weeks post-launch. **Bloom Pop**, a prebiotic/gut-health soda, launched exclusively at 3,000+ Walmart stores nationwide in July 2025. The beverage arm (energy drinks + soda) is on track to surpass the original supplement business in revenue this year. The freshest and hardest signal: beverage giant **Nutrabolt** (maker of C4), which first took a 20% minority stake for $90M in January 2024, invested an additional ~$160M in **September 2025**, taking majority ownership at a total commitment of ~$210M — explicit validation that the Sparkling Energy bet paid off.

**Acquisition channels:** Overwhelmingly influencer/creator-led — TikTok is essentially 100% of sponsored content volume, run through a self-reported 59,000-affiliate ambassador network of micro/mid-tier creators (fitness, motherhood, college niches) using UGC and discount-code conversion rather than traditional paid media. Retail is now the larger channel: CEO Greg LaVecchia states 75% of total sales come from retail partners — Target (600+ stores since 2022), Walmart, Sam's Club, GNC, and Ulta Beauty (300 stores as of July 2025).

**Retention/loyalty:** Subscribe & Save accounts for ~25% of digital revenue. The Bloom Wellness Club loyalty program ("seeds" per dollar, rewards for reviews/referrals) is explicitly designed to pull retail buyers back to the DTC site. Reported repeat-purchase rate ~12% above category norms.

**Marketing channels:** TikTok/Instagram/YouTube influencer and UGC content (12B+ cumulative views cited); founder-led personal brand built on Mari Llewellyn's own weight-loss story; retail co-marketing with Target/Walmart/Ulta; Nutrabolt's beverage-distribution network for the RTD lines.

**Geography:** Primarily US — DTC plus national mass retail; no material international expansion found.

**Model uniqueness:** Rather than raising traditional VC, Bloom financed its category expansion through a strategic-partner capital structure — Nutrabolt buying up to majority ownership in two tranches tied to proven product success, not a speculative round. It also diversified from a single hero SKU into a four-line portfolio faster than most DTC supplement peers, hedging against greens-powder category fatigue.

**Balance note:** Recurring criticism over influencer-disclosure transparency — some ambassador content allegedly uses the `#bloompartner` hashtag without clearer disclosure (Daily Dot). No third-party testing/FDA regulation independently verified, consistent with the DTC-supplement category norm. Sector-wide overhang: Consumer Reports' October 2025 heavy-metals investigation into protein powders/greens products raised general category scrutiny (not Bloom-specific). No lawsuits found against Bloom Nutrition specifically.
