## Section 2 — Brand Spotlight: TruHeight

TruHeight (legal entity Vanilla Chip LLC), a Las Vegas-based kids'/teen height-growth supplement brand founded in 2019 by Eden Stelmach and Justin Rapoport, had its breakout year in 2026 — built on TikTok Shop virality and a compressed pivot from pure DTC into national retail, with a genuinely consequential regulatory counterweight worth noting up front.

**Why it's grown.** Kalodata data (cited by trade press) named TruHeight the #1-selling kids' supplement brand on TikTok Shop in August 2026, with the company claiming its TikTok Shop monthly revenue "more than doubled." That momentum funded the company's largest retail expansion to date: a Target national debut in early 2026, ~5,000 CVS stores nationwide from June 2026, and Nordstrom/JCPenney marketplace listings from July 2026 — alongside Walmart, iHerb, and Amazon. A Vitamin Shoppe launch was announced in August for "this fall" but is unconfirmed by any newer source. On July 31, the brand extended beyond vitamins into functional food (Protein Energy Waffles), explicitly timed to a "World Cup summer" youth-sports surge, positioning itself as a broader "family nutrition company."

**Acquisition channels/tactics.** TikTok Shop creator/affiliate commerce is the primary engine, layered with a DTC subscription site, parent- and athlete-creator partnerships, and youth-sports sponsorships (presenting sponsor of the 2026 MSHTV youth basketball camp; repeat sponsor of creator Quincy Helsel's "Community Classic" in Denver).

**Retention/loyalty.** Auto-renew subscription — also the source of its sharpest consumer complaints (see below). A cause-marketing "Anti-Bullying Initiative" donates product to disadvantaged kids per purchase.

**Marketing channels.** Creator/testimonial-driven TikTok content, youth-sports sponsorship, mass-retail shelf placement.

**Geography.** Primarily US; Trustpilot storefronts exist in several EU markets (Germany, Switzerland, Denmark) but no confirmed international retail presence.

**What makes the model unique.** An unusually fast single-SKU-DTC-subscription → mass-retail → adjacent-category pivot, compressed into roughly seven months.

**Balance note — significant, not a footnote.** The FTC brought a case (announced April 2026, final order approved July 2026) against TruHeight and its co-founders over unsubstantiated "grow up to 6 inches/year" claims, thousands of employee-written fake five-star reviews, and bot social accounts — $750K payment plus a permanent ad-claims bar. A parallel class action (*Gonzalez v. Vanilla Chip LLC*, SDNY, filed March 2026) alleges TruHeight's own sponsored study showed under-10 control-group kids grew as much or more without the product. BBB rating: D, 36 complaints. Trustpilot: mixed, ~3.5, recurring subscription-cancellation complaints.

**Runner-ups considered:** Grüns (closest in scale, but its headline numbers trace to the April 9 Unilever-deal announcement — pre-window by this brief's "trailing 6-12 months" freshness standard — and the deal's actual closing date remains unconfirmed; this week's only fresh Grüns fact is a September class action over "sugar-free" labeling, litigation rather than growth); Tiny Health ($33M Series B, Oct 1 — fresh, but a microbiome-diagnostics company selling supplements as a test follow-on, with no disclosed revenue/growth figure).

**Sources:** [Chain Drug Review (TikTok Shop #1)](https://chaindrugreview.com/truheight-named-the-1-selling-kids-supplement-brand-on-tiktok-shop/), [Chain Drug Review (CVS launch)](https://chaindrugreview.com/truheight-launches-in-5-000-cvs-stores-nationwide/), [Eastern Progress (Nordstrom/JCPenney)](https://www.easternprogress.com/truheight-joins-nordstrom-and-jcpenney-marketplaces-as-wellness-brands-reshape-the-department-store/article_68186c98-36cd-51ea-adf0-4e0bf2607c8d.html), [Youth Sports Business Report (Protein Waffles)](https://youthsportsbusinessreport.com/truheight-expands-into-youth-sports-nutrition-with-protein-waffles-as-fall-registrations-climb/), [FTC final order](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-approves-final-order-against-truheight-deceptive-unsubstantiated-advertising-supplements-kids), [class action complaint](https://www.classaction.org/media/truheight-complaint.pdf), [AOL/BBB rating](https://www.aol.com/boys-falling-truheight-supplement-promising-100007193.html), [Trustpilot](https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/truheightvitamins.com).
